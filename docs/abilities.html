Custom Weapons — the ten legendaries

Three of each exist on the whole server, found in temples and claimed at their altars. Every one matches or beats its vanilla base on raw damage, and the ability is on top. Right-click = the active ability; the rest is passive.

Bloodletter netherite sword

4.0 damage · 2.0 speed · 8.0 DPS, ~11 with Rage · Rage 30 s
  • Rage (right-click, 30 s): Strength I for 10 s. No target needed.
  • A normal swing does not bleed any more.
  • Bleed — only from the ultimate Crimson Nova (sneak + left-click): everything within 6 blocks takes 3 hearts of true damage and starts bleeding at full stacks — 1.0 every 0.5 s, 9.0 in all — and you heal ½ heart per victim.
  • One bleed per victim; bleed kills count for whoever applied it. Cleared by milk, death or changing dimension.

Gale Edge diamond sword

7.0 damage · 1.8 speed · 12.6 DPS, +4.0 after a dash · 8 s cooldown
  • Dash (right-click, 8 s): launches you along your look direction (upward angle capped, so looking up isn't a rocket). 6 s of fall immunity.
  • Momentum Strike: the next hit within 2 s of a dash does +4.0.
  • One dash in the air. Airborne with it spent, the click does nothing and doesn't use the cooldown.

Stormpiercer bow

10.0 at full draw (no crit roll), +12.0 with the shock · 15 s cooldown · unbreakable · cannot be enchanted
  • A fully drawn arrow hits for exactly 10.0 before armour; partial draws scale down.
  • Shock: when ready, a fully drawn hit does 22.0, calls lightning down on the target, gives Glowing for 6 s, and arcs 6.0 to up to three other creatures within 6 blocks, stunning them too. Charged on the hit, so a miss costs nothing.
  • Stun: the shocked target is rooted for 3 s — can't move or jump.
  • Execute: a fully drawn hit kills a creeper or a skeleton outright (never players). Normal loot and XP.
  • The bolt is visual by default: flash and thunder, no fire, no charged creepers.
  • No enchantments at all — you trade Power, Flame and Infinity for the shock.

Aegis Hammer netherite axe

11.0 damage · 0.9 speed · 9.9 DPS, slam 8.0 · 15 s cooldown
  • Ground Slam (right-click on the ground, 15 s): 8.0 damage, Slowness II for 4 s and a pop-up to everything within 5 blocks that can see you; Resistance I for 5 s for you. Does nothing in mid-air.
  • Stagger (passive): every third hit within 3 s of the last stuns the target for 1 s — rooted, can't jump, still takes knockback.
  • It's an axe, so it still disables shields like vanilla.

Frostbrand iron sword

8.0 damage · 1.6 speed · 12.8 DPS, shatter +8.0 · beam 30 s
  • Ice Beam (right-click, 30 s): a ray of ice up to 24 blocks. The first thing it touches takes 6.0 and is frozen solid for 3 s — can't move, jump or be knocked back — then the ice bursts. A beam that hits nothing still costs the cooldown.
  • Frost (passive): every hit adds freeze (the powder-snow effect: screen frosts over, mobs shiver) and Slowness II for 2 s.
  • Shatter: three hits in a row freeze the target; the third does +8.0 and Slowness IV for 2 s, then the frost resets. Hit faster than they thaw.
  • Absolute Zero (sneak + left-click, 45 s): everything within 7 blocks takes 3 hearts of true damage and is frozen solid for 4 s — then the thaw shatters the ice: +4 true damage, Slowness III and Weakness for 5 s.

Tidecaller trident

10.0 damage · 1.1 speed · 11.0 DPS, +4.0 on wet targets · 10 s cooldown
  • Harpoon: a thrown hit drags the target to you. 10 s cooldown, charged on the hit — a miss costs nothing. Loyalty and Riptide still work.
  • Tide (passive): thrown or swung, anything standing in water or rain takes +4.0.

Hellfire crossbow

bolt + blast · 8 s cooldown
  • Exploding bolt: the first bolt after the cooldown explodes where it lands — on a creature or in the ground. About 15 before armour on a direct hit, falling off fast. Charged on the shot.
  • Breaks no blocks. It does hurt you at your own feet like TNT would.
  • Multishot: one blast per volley, not three.

Dawnbreaker golden sword

9.0 damage, +2.0 by day · 1.7 speed · 15.3 DPS by day, sunstrike 9.0 · 15 s cooldown · unbreakable
  • Sunstrike (right-click, 15 s): the spot you're looking at (block or creature, up to 24 blocks) is marked, and one second later a pillar of sunlight lands: 9.0 to everything within 3 blocks, double to the undead, sets them alight for 4 s. Looking at the sky does nothing and costs nothing.
  • Solar Brand (passive): every hit burns for 3 s; while it's bright outside every hit does +2.0.
  • Daylight (passive): a kill heals you 4.0.

Voidreaper netherite hoe — a scythe

10.0 damage · 1.3 speed · 13.0 DPS, backstab +6.0 · 12 s cooldown
  • Rift (right-click, 12 s): the first creature along your look within 12 blocks is marked; you're torn through the void and appear right behind it, facing it. Nothing marked = nothing happens, no cooldown.
  • Backstab: your next hit within 2 s of a Rift does +6.0.
  • Wither (passive): every hit withers for 2 s.
  • Soul Harvest (passive): a kill heals you 4.0 and gives 4 absorption for 10 s.

Starfall mace

8.0 ×2 damage · 0.7 speed · 11.2 DPS, impact 8.0 in 4 blocks · 20 s cooldown
  • Comet (right-click from the ground, 20 s): you're launched about 14 blocks straight up with 6 s of fall immunity, falling as a comet for 5 s.
  • Impact: while falling as a comet, your first hit or touching the ground is the impact — 8.0 to everything within 4 blocks, knocked away and set alight for 2 s. The vanilla mace smash still lands on top.
  • Heavy (passive): every hit shoves the target half a block further along your swing.
  • Double Strike (passive): every hit lands twice — the second hit is separate, so MaceCap's 4-heart smash cap doesn't apply to it. Smash from height = smash + impact + swing + swing again.
